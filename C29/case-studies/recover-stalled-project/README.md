# Case study 3: Recover a stalled AI project

*About 2 hours. No code. You need the [project file](project-file.md), the [evidence card](evidence-card.md), and the [stage-gate roadmap](../../templates/stage-gate-roadmap.md), [definition of done](../../templates/definition-of-done.md) and [role map](../../templates/role-map.md) templates.*

## The situation

Six months ago, Larkfield started a project to warn customers before a delivery is late. The sponsor has since left the company. The project has spent its budget, the team says it is "nearly ready" every month, and nobody outside the team has used it. Nadia, the new head of customer operations, now owns it. She asks you: what went wrong, and what should happen in the next 90 days? Continue, change it, or stop it?

The whole case is **constructed** for the course: the project, its people's words and its numbers. It is built from the mistakes that the course teaches you to see.

## What you do

1. **Read the [project file](project-file.md)** from start to end, and mark every place where a decision should have been made and was not.
2. **Diagnose.** Write the three to five causes of the stall, each with the evidence from the file (a quote, a number, a missing document). Separate causes from symptoms.
3. **Decide what to keep.** What in the project still has value (data, people, a finding)? What is waste that you must stop paying for?
4. **Write a 90-day recovery plan** with the [stage-gate roadmap](../../templates/stage-gate-roadmap.md): an owner who decides, a clear problem, a baseline that is not AI, an agreed evaluation set, a definition of done, an owner for adoption, and a stop result at every gate.
5. **Write one paragraph for Nadia**: continue, change or stop, and why.

## What a good plan does

- It names a single decision owner and a domain owner (the people whose work changes).
- It compares the model with a simple rule or report before it spends more on the model.
- It fixes the evaluation set and the threshold before anyone reports a new score.
- It has gates with a time box and a result that stops the work, and "stop" is a real option.
- It plans adoption: who will use the warning, how customers receive it, and how success is measured (fewer "where is my order?" tickets, not "the model is accurate").

## Make it a portfolio piece

Publish your diagnosis (one page) and your 90-day plan (one page). Say that the project is a constructed course case. Recovering a stalled project is common work for product leads and engineering managers; a short, honest diagnosis shows that you can see the pattern and act on it.
