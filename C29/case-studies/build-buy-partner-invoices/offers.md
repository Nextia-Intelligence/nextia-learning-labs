# The four offers

*All four offers, the companies, prices and terms are **constructed** for the case study. No real company is meant.*

| | Build in-house | ScanLedger | FormGrid | Northgate Analytics (partner) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Rules first, a vision model only where the rules fail, a check that sends errors to a clerk | A vision model reads every page and gives a confidence per value | Templates per document layout; documents it cannot read go to a clerk | A specialist firm builds and runs extraction in Larkfield's cloud account |
| Price per document | the model's cost on the documents the rules cannot read (recorded: US$0.0018 on average) | US$0.06 | US$0.01 | US$0.15 |
| Monthly fee | none (but engineering upkeep: an assumption) | US$0 | US$450 | US$1,200 |
| One-time cost | the build: an assumption | US$0 | US$3,000 set-up + US$400 per new layout | US$15,000 |
| Contract | — | monthly | monthly | 36 months |
| Their evidence | the trial below | "99.6% field accuracy" on the vendor's own 500 sample invoices | the trial below | a case study from a hardware wholesaler: '98% straight-through'; no result on Larkfield's documents |
| Your data | documents stay in Larkfield's systems; the vision model's provider keeps no data under the course's settings (check the provider's terms) | documents kept 30 days; may be used to improve the service unless you opt out in writing; stored in United States | processed in Canada; deleted after 24 hours; never used for training | documents stay in Larkfield's cloud account; the partner's staff get access to it |
| If you leave | Larkfield owns the code and the rules; it must keep people who can change them | results by API in JSON; no bulk export of documents after 30 days | templates belong to the vendor; CSV export of results | the partner builds in Larkfield's account; code handed over at the end of the contract |
