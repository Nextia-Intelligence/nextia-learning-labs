# Omar's proposal

*The proposal is **constructed** for the case study. The numbers in it are **recorded** (in the pilot evidence pack), but Omar chose which ones to show.*

---

**From:** Omar (product manager)
**To:** Nadia (head of customer operations)
**Subject:** Next step for the support reply assistant: three changes

Nadia,

The pilot went well. I propose three changes, to start on Monday.

**1. Switch to the new prompt (v3) for every ticket.** It is shorter: about 22% fewer words in every request, so it should cut our model bill. Its replies are warmer: when the evaluation team asked a strong model to compare the replies side by side, it preferred v3's reply 126 times and v2's only 48 times.

**2. Move agents from "edit the draft" to "approve or reject".** Agents spend a lot of time on drafts that are already fine. If they only approve, the review takes about 0.6 minutes per ticket instead of editing.

**3. Give the assistant to all agents**, not only the pilot team.

The model costs almost nothing (US$0.34 per 1,000 tickets today), so the risk is small. Can you approve on Friday?

Omar
