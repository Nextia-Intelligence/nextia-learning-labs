## Check your map

- Every column has exactly one D.
- The domain expert is consulted or decides on quality at every stage.
- Someone owns the evaluation set, and it is not the person whose work is evaluated.
- Someone owns adoption: the people who will use the product.
- Someone answers at night when it fails.
