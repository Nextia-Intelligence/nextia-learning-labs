## 3. Questions to send to each vendor

1. What do you keep from our data (inputs, outputs, logs), where, for how long, and do you use it to train or improve any model?
2. Will you run a trial on our own examples, scored by us, before we sign? Which examples did your claims use?
3. What does the service cost at our volume (low, base, high), with every fee: set-up, minimums, overage, support?
4. How do we leave? What can we export, in which format, and with how much notice?
5. What happens when your model changes? How do you tell us, and can we test the change first?
6. What fails most often, and what do you do then?
