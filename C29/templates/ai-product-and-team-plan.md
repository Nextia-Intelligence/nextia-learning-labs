# AI product and team plan (final assignment)

*Write a plan for one use case in your own field, or one you know well. Fill each section; link each claim to evidence, or label it an assumption. Each section names the template that helps. A reviewer marks each required item **demonstrated**, **partially demonstrated** or **not yet demonstrated**, with a reason; you may revise and send it again.*

**Use case:** **Your role:** **Field:**

## 1. The problem and its value
- The user, the task, the cost of a mistake, and why AI is the right tool (not a rule, a report or a process change). *([use-case screen](use-case-screen.md))*
- A value range (low, base, high) with its main assumptions, and the assumption that changes it most. *([value estimate](value-estimate.md))*

## 2. Build, buy or partner
- The options, the criteria written first, the evidence for each score, and the decision. *([vendor comparison](vendor-comparison.md))*
- The fair trial that supports it, or the one you would run. *([pilot plan](pilot-plan.md))*

## 3. Roadmap and definition of done
- Stage gates with time boxes, the evidence to pass and the stop result at each gate. *([stage-gate roadmap](stage-gate-roadmap.md))*
- Acceptance thresholds on a named evaluation set, and who signs off. *([definition of done](definition-of-done.md))*

## 4. People: human review and adoption
- The autonomy level, justified with the cost of a mistake and the cost of review. *([autonomy ladder](autonomy-ladder.md), [human-review canvas](human-review-canvas.md))*
- The adoption plan, with an outcome measure. *([adoption plan](adoption-plan.md))*

## 5. The team
- Roles by stage, with who decides. *([role map](role-map.md))*
- How you review work you cannot write yourself. *([experiment-review checklist](experiment-review-checklist.md))*
- The team's policy for AI-assisted work. *([policy](ai-assisted-development-policy.md))*

## 6. Risk and launch
- The risk register with owners. *([risk register](risk-register.md))*
- Launch steps, the signals you watch and the pause conditions. *([launch checklist](launch-checklist.md))*

---

## Required evidence (the reviewer's list)

| # | Required item | Demonstrated when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A use case with a value range and its main assumptions, and the reason AI is the right tool | The range has low, base and high; every input has a label; the biggest assumption is named; a simpler fix was considered |
| 2 | A build, buy or partner decision tied to stated criteria and trial evidence | Criteria were written before scoring; every option is judged on the same evidence; a demo alone is not the evidence |
| 3 | A stage-gate roadmap with acceptance thresholds and stop criteria | Every gate has the evidence to pass and a result that stops the work; thresholds are numbers on a named set, with an owner |
| 4 | A human-review and adoption design with a justified level of autonomy | The level is justified with the cost of a mistake and of review; the adoption plan measures an outcome, not only usage |
| 5 | A team plan with roles, review practices and an AI-assisted development policy | One decider per stage; review questions that work without code; a policy that says who owns the result |
| 6 | A risk register with owners, and launch and pause conditions | Every high-impact risk has an owner; pause conditions are written; a person can correct the AI where a mistake causes harm |

**Not accepted:** a plan that launches without an owner for a high-impact risk, or without a way for a person to correct the AI where a mistake causes harm.
