# A plan with fixed dates: website product chat

*This plan is **constructed** for the course's practice in Module 3. Turn it into a stage-gate roadmap with the [stage-gate roadmap](../templates/stage-gate-roadmap.md) template.*

---

**Project:** A chat on Larkfield's website that answers customers' questions about products (sizes, materials, care, what fits with what), from the product pages and manuals.
**Sponsor:** Omar. **Team:** Amira (developer), Tomás (AI engineer), half of Mei's time (data engineer).
**Budget:** 12 weeks of the team's time.

| Week | Milestone | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Choose the model and build the chat | The chat answers on the test website |
| 3–4 | Connect all product pages and manuals | All 2,400 products are in |
| 5–6 | Make the answers better | The team is happy with the answers |
| 7 | Design and accessibility review | The chat looks like the website |
| 8 | Train the support team | Agents know the chat exists |
| 9 | **Launch to all customers** | The chat is on every product page |
| 10–12 | Improvements from customer feedback | — |

**Success:** customers love the chat, and fewer customers write tickets about products.

**Risks:** the model provider could raise prices.

---

## Questions to ask about this plan

1. Which week tells the team whether the chat can answer product questions **well enough**? What is "well enough"?
2. What result in which week would **stop** the project or change it?
3. Who decides that the chat is ready to launch, and with what evidence?
4. What happens to a customer who gets a wrong answer about a product's safety or size?
5. How would you know, in week 12, if the chat reduced product tickets?
