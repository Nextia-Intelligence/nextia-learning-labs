# Two vendor proposals for the support reply assistant

*Both vendors, their products, prices, claims and terms are **constructed** for the course. No real company is meant. Larkfield's own numbers come from the [pilot evidence pack](pilot-evidence-pack/README.md).*

Omar asked two vendors how they would give Larkfield's agents draft replies. These are their one-page summaries, as they sent them.

## Proposal A: ReplyRight

> **A help-desk add-on that drafts replies with its own hosted model.**
>
> - **Price:** US$39 per agent per month, for your 12 agents. Set-up: US$2,500. Contract: 24 months.
> - **Results:** 92% of drafts sent without edits.
> - **How we know:** a demo on 20 tickets that the vendor chose, from another shop.
> - **Trial on your own tickets before you sign:** not offered.
> - **Your data:** tickets and replies kept 18 months; used to improve the vendor's model unless you buy the Enterprise plan. Data stored in: United States.
> - **If you leave:** export of settings only; no export of the reply history.

## Proposal B: DraftDesk

> **A drafting service that uses a large hosted model and your policy documents.**
>
> - **Price:** US$4.00 per 1,000 tickets, at least US$300 a month. Set-up: US$6,000. Contract: 12 months.
> - **Results:** Pilot on 200 of your own tickets before you sign; you keep the scored results.
> - **How we know:** none yet: the pilot is the evidence.
> - **Trial on your own tickets before you sign:** yes.
> - **Your data:** deleted 30 days after the reply; never used for training. Data stored in: Canada.
> - **If you leave:** full export of replies and settings in a standard format; 60 days' notice.

## What Larkfield has today (recorded)

Larkfield's own assistant (prompt v2) costs US$0.34 in model fees per 1,000 tickets, sends 24 of 24 holdout tickets that need a person to a person, and answers 98 of 98 holdout tickets. Its people cost (engineering upkeep) is an assumption in [Review time and cost](pilot-evidence-pack/3-review-time-and-cost.md).
