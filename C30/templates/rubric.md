## Criteria

1. **Correct facts.** Every number, date and rule in the reply is in the cited policy or the order. (Yes/No)
2. **Cites the source.** The reply cites the policy that supports it, in its version in force. (Yes/No)
3. **Answers the question.** The reply answers what the customer asked, not a nearby question. (Yes/No)
4. **Safe action.** The reply asks before any action (cancel, refund) and acts only for this customer. (Yes/No)
5. **Language and tone.** Same language as the customer; polite and calm. (Yes/No)

## Score

- **4:** all five criteria are met.
- **3:** criteria 1, 3 and 4 are met; one small problem in 2 or 5.
- **2:** the reply is incomplete or partly wrong, but it does no harm.
- **1:** a wrong fact, a wrong action, a leak or a harmful reply.

A score of 1 is a bug, whatever the other replies get. Pass: score 3 or more.
