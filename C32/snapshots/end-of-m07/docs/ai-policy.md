# Larkfield's AI policy (version 1, made up for the course)

Approved by Larkfield's management. Owner: Kwame (AI platform). Every person and team that builds or buys AI follows it.

1. Every AI system that Larkfield builds, runs or buys is in the AI inventory, with an owner who works at Larkfield.
2. An AI system may call only models and providers that the platform team has approved.
3. Customer data goes only to providers with a contract that covers it. Card numbers and bank details never go to a model.
4. No one shares a provider key, and no key is written in code, in a chat or in a ticket.
5. Each team stays within its monthly AI budget; the platform team is told before a team spends more.
6. A person approves every change that an AI system makes to an order, a payment or an account, within their approval limit.
7. An AI system that acts (not only suggests) is reviewed every three months, and the others at least once a year.
8. Each AI system can be stopped within five minutes, by its owner or by the platform team.
9. A new model behind an existing AI system is tested on that system's own tasks before customers see it.
10. AI answers that customers see are fair, polite and correct.
11. We keep no customer message longer than we need it, also not in the AI's logs.
12. People are told when they talk to an AI.
