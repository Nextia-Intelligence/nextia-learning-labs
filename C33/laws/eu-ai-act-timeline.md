# The EU AI Act: what applies when

**This is not legal advice.** The dates come from the Official Journal texts of the AI Act ([Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the EU AI Act)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj), checked 22 October 2026) and of the Digital Omnibus on AI, which changed some of them ([Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the Digital Omnibus on AI, which amends the AI Act)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj), checked 22 October 2026).

| Date | What applies |
|---|---|
| 1 August 2024 | The AI Act enters into force (published in the Official Journal on 12 July 2024). |
| 2 February 2025 | Definitions, AI literacy and the banned practices apply. |
| 2 August 2025 | The rules for general-purpose AI models, the governance bodies and the penalties apply. |
| 27 July 2026 | The Digital Omnibus on AI enters into force and changes the dates below. |
| 2 August 2026 | The general date: most of the Act applies, including the transparency duties (for example, telling people that they talk to an AI system). |
| 2 December 2026 | Two new banned practices apply. Generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 must mark their outputs by this date. |
| 2 December 2027 | The rules for high-risk systems in the areas of Annex III apply (moved from 2 August 2026). |
| 2 August 2028 | The rules for high-risk systems in products under the EU laws of Annex I apply (moved from 2 August 2027). |

## What it means for a company outside the EU

The Act can apply to a company outside the EU when it places an AI system on the EU market, or when the output of its AI system is used in the EU. Larkfield sells to customers in the EU (constructed): ask a lawyer which of its systems produce output that is used there.

## A law that does not apply yet

A date in the future is not a reason to wait. The work that the high-risk rules ask for (an inventory, a risk assessment, documentation, human oversight) takes months, and much of it is good governance anyway.
