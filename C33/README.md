# AI Governance: practice files

These are the practice files of the course [AI Governance: Policies, Risk and Accountability](https://learning.nextia-ai.com/courses/ai-governance/). You need a browser and a document tool (a word processor, a text editor or a spreadsheet). There is no code, no account and no cost.

**This course is not legal advice.** The files explain laws and standards in plain words, each with its official source and the date it was checked. Ask a lawyer about your own organisation.

## The situation

Larkfield is a home-and-garden shop. It is made up for the course. It sells to customers in Canada and in the European Union (constructed: this makes both the EU AI Act and Canadian privacy law relevant). Larkfield uses AI in eight places: four systems that its teams built, one script that nobody registered, and three AI features inside tools that it buys. You follow Farida (she), Larkfield's first risk and compliance lead.

## Every number has a label

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **recorded** | Measured by an earlier course of Nextia Learning, in a committed run. The file names the course and links it. |
| **official source** | A law, a standard or a regulator's page. The file links it, quotes at most a short phrase and gives the date it was checked. |
| **assumption** | A value we chose, with the reason. Change it and the result changes. |
| **constructed** | Written for this course: Larkfield's people, policies, vendors, requests, incidents and complaints. The vendors and their answers are made up; no real company is meant. |

## Check your files

Open `portfolio/README.md`. It lists eight systems. If you see fewer, download the files again. To start again at any time, download a fresh copy: nothing here changes by itself.
