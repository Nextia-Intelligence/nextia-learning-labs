# Final assignment: Larkfield's AI governance pack for its next year

You are Farida. Nadia asks for one pack that the board can adopt: what Larkfield runs, who owns it, the rules, the controls and the plan for the next 90 days. Use the files of this folder; every template you need is in `../templates/`.

**This is not legal advice.** Every legal statement in your pack needs its official source and the date you checked it; say what you would ask a lawyer.

## What the pack contains

| # | Part | Built from | Demonstrated when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A complete inventory with owners and risk tiers, and a responsibility table | `inventory.csv`, `risk-tier-table.md`, `responsibility-table.md` | All 8 systems have a named accountable owner and a tier with its reason; the responsibility table covers at least the systems of the medium and high tiers |
| 2 | One impact assessment for a high-tier system | `impact-assessment.md` | Every section is filled with evidence, and the decision has conditions with owners |
| 3 | An acceptable-use policy and a policy for building and buying AI | `acceptable-use-policy-draft.md`, `build-and-buy-policy.md` | Each is tested with three "may I...?" questions that it answers alone |
| 4 | A vendor decision with reasons, and a control map with the evidence for each control | `vendor-answers.md`, `control-map.md` | The decision names the gaps and the conditions; every control has an owner and evidence that exists without extra work |
| 5 | The board's terms of reference, one incident decision, and a 90-day plan with measures | `board/`, `incidents/`, `governance-scorecard.md` | The plan has at most eight actions, each with an owner and a measure from the scorecard |

## Rules for the whole pack

- No legal statement without its official source and the date checked.
- Every number keeps its label: recorded, official source, assumption, constructed.
