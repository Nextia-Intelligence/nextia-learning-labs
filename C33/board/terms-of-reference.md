# AI governance board: terms of reference (template)

> A template, with Larkfield's first version filled in (constructed). Change it to fit your organisation.

| Part | Larkfield's version |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Decide on AI uses that are high tier or that the fast path cannot decide; keep the inventory, the risks and the exceptions under control; learn from incidents. |
| Chair | Nadia (executive sponsor) |
| Members | Farida (risk and compliance, secretary), Grace (customer promise and refunds), Mei (data and privacy), Kwame (platform controls), the finance lead; the accountable owner of each system on the agenda attends for that item |
| Asked when needed | The outside lawyer; security |
| Decides | High-tier launches and changes; exceptions to policy; vendors for medium- and high-tier uses; what to change after an incident |
| Does not decide | Low-tier requests (fast path: the owner and the risk lead, within 2 working days); medium-tier changes that pass their gates (the accountable owner, with a decision record) |
| Rhythm | Every two weeks, 45 minutes; an extra meeting within 2 working days for a serious incident |
| Time a decision should take | 10 working days at most for a complete request; a request that is not complete is sent back within 2 days, with what is missing |
| Records | Minutes with each decision, its reason and its conditions; a decision record per decision; the scorecard each quarter |
| Quorum | The chair or the risk lead, and at least three members |
| Review of these terms | Every year, and after the first quarter |
