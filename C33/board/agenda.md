# Board agenda (template)

45 minutes. Papers go out two working days before the meeting; a request without its papers moves to the next meeting.

| # | Item | Time | Papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incidents and complaints since the last meeting: what happened, what we changed | 10 min | incident and complaint records |
| 2 | Decisions: requests that only the board can decide | 20 min | each request with its tier, impact assessment or vendor answers, and the owner's recommendation |
| 3 | Fast-path decisions since the last meeting (for information, not for approval) | 5 min | list |
| 4 | Scorecard, open risks and exceptions near their end date | 5 min | scorecard |
| 5 | Rule watch: changes in laws and standards | 5 min | the map of laws and standards |
