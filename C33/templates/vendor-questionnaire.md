# Vendor and model questionnaire

Send it before you buy or switch on an AI feature. A good answer is specific: a number, a name, a contract clause. "Industry standard", "without undue delay" and "leading models" are not answers.

| # | Topic | Question | What a good answer looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data use | What do you do with our data, apart from giving us the service? | "Only to provide the service", written in the contract |
| 2 | Training | Do you or your providers train models on our data? Is that on or off by default? | "No", or "off by default", in the contract, not an e-mail opt-out |
| 3 | Retention | How long do you keep our data, the prompts and the outputs? | A number of days for each kind |
| 4 | Sub-processors | Which companies process our data for you, including the model provider? | A named list, and notice before a new one is added |
| 5 | Location | In which countries is our data stored and processed? | Named countries; whether that can change |
| 6 | Security | What independent evidence of security can you show? | A named audit report we may read |
| 7 | Incident notice | How fast do you tell us about an incident that touches our data? | A number of hours, in the contract |
| 8 | Model information | Which model is used, what is it known to do badly, and do you tell us before it changes? | A name or a model card, and notice of changes |
| 9 | Human oversight | What can our staff see and correct before an output is used? | A description of the product's controls, not of our staff |
| 10 | Exit | What happens to our data when we leave, and what can we export? | Deletion within a number of days; export formats |

**Labels:** the questions are the course's own. Larkfield's two vendors and their answers are constructed ([vendor answers](../vendors/vendor-answers.md)).
