# Which rules apply to which system

For each system, write which laws, standards or contracts may apply, the role Larkfield has under each (for the EU AI Act: provider, deployer or both), the reason, and the questions for a lawyer. Use the [map of laws and standards](../laws/map-of-laws-and-standards.md). **This is not legal advice**: the goal is to know what to ask, and whom.

Larkfield sells to customers in Canada and in the European Union (constructed).

## Worked example: Document assistant

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is the output used in the EU? | Not directly: staff in Canada read the answers. The partner shop's staff use it too: where are they? Unknown. |
| EU AI Act category (the course's four-level picture) | Most likely minimal risk: it answers staff from documents and is not in a high-risk area. It is not a chatbot that customers talk to. |
| Role under the EU AI Act | Larkfield built it, so it would be the provider; it also uses it, so it would be the deployer. |
| Privacy law | Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) applies to personal information in the documents; Larkfield stays accountable when a vendor processes it. |
| Contracts | The model provider's contract (data use, retention, location). |
| Standards and frameworks | None required. ISO/IEC 42001 or the NIST AI RMF only if Larkfield chooses them. |
| Not sure | Where the partner shop's staff are, and whether the answers ever reach EU customers. |
| Questions for a lawyer | Does serving a partner shop's staff make Larkfield a provider to another company under the AI Act? |
