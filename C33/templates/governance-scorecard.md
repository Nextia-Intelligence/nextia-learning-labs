# Governance scorecard

A short set of measures that shows whether governance helps: are systems known and owned, are decisions fast where risk is low, is risk going down? Read the measures together: one measure alone can be gamed.

| Measure | How to count | Good sign | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inventory coverage | Systems in the registry ÷ systems known from every source | Rising to all | Bought and found systems missing |
| Systems with a named accountable owner | Count, and the list of those without | All | A team or a role instead of a name |
| Time to decision, by tier and path | Median working days from request to decision | Low tier on the fast path in days | Low tier waiting at the board as long as high tier |
| Share of decisions that are not a plain "yes" | Rejected, sent back or approved with conditions ÷ decided | Some, for good reasons | 0: approvals that never say no |
| Overdue reviews | Systems past their review date | 0 | Growing |
| Open risks, and open risks without an owner | Count | Falling; every risk owned | Risks with no owner |
| Exceptions | Open; past their end date | Few, each with an end date | Exceptions that never end |
| Staff trained on acceptable use | Share of staff | Rising | Low while use grows |
