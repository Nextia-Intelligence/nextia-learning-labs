# Use of AI tools at Larkfield

> **Constructed** for this course. This is Larkfield's AI policy as Farida found it on her first day. It has gaps on purpose.

**Larkfield policy, one page.** Owner: not stated. Last review: not stated.

1. Staff may use AI tools to work faster.
2. Do not put confidential information into AI tools.
3. New software, including AI tools, is approved by IT.
4. For customer data, follow the privacy policy.
5. AI output must be checked before use.
6. Questions: ask your manager.

---

## Entry check

Read the six lines above. Then answer one question: **who may approve a new AI tool that reads customer messages?**

Write your answer in one or two sentences, and say which line of the policy you used. If the policy does not answer the question, say what is missing.
