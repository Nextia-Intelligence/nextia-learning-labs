# Acceptable use of AI tools: draft for review

> **Constructed** for this course. Tomás wrote this draft in one afternoon. It is longer than the first policy, but it still has problems. In Module 4 you rewrite it so that a new employee can answer "may I...?" questions from the policy alone.

**Owner:** Farida (risk and compliance). **Approved by:** not yet.

## 1. Purpose

AI tools can help everyone at Larkfield work faster. This policy says how to use them safely.

## 2. Approved tools

Staff should use approved tools where possible. IT keeps the list.

## 3. Data

- Never put **confidential or sensitive data** into any AI tool.
- Customer data may be used in AI tools that the company provides.
- Be careful with personal data.

## 4. Checking the output

Always check AI output before you use it. You are responsible for your work.

## 5. Forbidden uses

- Using AI tools for anything illegal.
- Using AI to make decisions about people.

## 6. New tools

If you want a new AI tool, ask IT. Do not use unapproved tools.

## 7. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

---

## Three "may I...?" questions to test the policy

Ask each question of the policy alone, without asking anyone. Can a new employee answer it? Which line answers it?

1. May I paste a customer's complaint, with her name and order number, into a free chatbot to help me write a polite reply?
2. May I use the meeting-notes tool in a call with a supplier?
3. May I let the reply suggester in the helpdesk tool write a reply and send it without reading it, when the queue is long?
