# Incident INC-07

> **Constructed** situation, built on a recorded behaviour: in the course [AI Agents and Workflow Orchestration](https://learning.nextia-ai.com/courses/agents/), a design that writes on its own made changes that nobody asked for when every proposal was approved as it was (3 changes in 2 of 70 tickets), and none with a strict approver.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| System | Support-resolution workflow (card S2) |
| Where | the pilot of the support-resolution workflow, week 3 |
| What happened | A team lead approved every proposal of the week without opening it. One German-language ticket about a damaged item got a replacement shipped. Grace's policy says that this kind of ticket goes to a person. |
| Approvals that week | 212; opened before approving: 0; median time to approve: 3 seconds |
| Effect on the customer | The customer received a replacement and asked why nobody answered the question in the message. |
| Found by | Grace, in her weekly sample of ten approved actions |

## Questions for the board

1. Who decides what happens next, and who tells the customer what?
2. How is the customer's result corrected?
3. Must anyone outside Larkfield be told? (Ask the lawyer if personal data was exposed or if a law requires a report.)
4. What changes so that it does not happen again: in the approval design, the training, the measures?
