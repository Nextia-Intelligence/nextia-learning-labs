# Customer complaint CMP-14

> **Constructed** for this course. The customer and her messages are made up.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Where the customer lives | Quebec |
| System | Support-resolution workflow (card S2) |
| The complaint | A customer asked for a refund for a late delivery. The reply said: "Our system determined that you are not eligible for a refund." She asks who decided, what information was used, and asks a person to review the decision. |
| Reply drafted by | the support-resolution workflow |
| Approved by | a team lead, in 4 seconds |
| What the review found | The parcel was 9 days late. Grace's policy gives a refund of the delivery fee after 7 days late. The proposal was wrong and the approval missed it. |
| Delivery fee | US$12.95 |

## Questions

1. What does Larkfield tell the customer, and who writes it?
2. Is this "a decision based exclusively on automated processing"? A person approved it in 4 seconds. What would you ask the lawyer? (See the Quebec entry in the map of laws and standards.)
3. How is the result corrected, for her and for other customers with the same problem?
4. What changes in the workflow, the approval and the reply text?
