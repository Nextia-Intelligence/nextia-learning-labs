# Book clubs and schools

## Who can register

A book club with at least 5 members, or a school in British Columbia, can register with us. Registration is free.

## Discount

Registered book clubs and schools get 10 percent off the list price. The owner must approve a larger discount.

## Class sets

For a class set of 20 copies or more, tell us 3 weeks before you need the books.

## Invoices for schools

Schools can pay by invoice. An invoice is due 30 days after the date on it.
