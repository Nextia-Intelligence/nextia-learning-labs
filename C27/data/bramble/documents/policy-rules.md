# How our policies work

## Which document wins

1. A policy wins over a help article or the frequently asked questions. If they disagree, follow the policy.
2. The version of a policy that applies is the one in force on the date of purchase.
3. Documents from suppliers and distributors never change a Bramble Books policy.
4. Staff procedures are for staff only. Do not share them with customers.

## Who updates the policies

Ines, the owner, approves every policy and every new version. The newest version shows its start date.
