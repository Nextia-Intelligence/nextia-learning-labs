# Loyalty stamp card

## How it works

You get 1 stamp for each book you buy at full price.

| Stamps | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | A free tote bag |
| 10 | 10 dollars off your next purchase |
| 20 | 25 dollars off your next purchase |

## When stamps expire

Stamps expire 12 months after your first stamp on the card.

## What does not count

Gift cards, special-order deposits and event tickets do not get stamps.
