# Delivery times

## Shipping

We ship within 2 business days. Delivery in British Columbia takes 2 to 4 business days, and 4 to 8 days to the rest of Canada. We do not ship outside Canada.

## Delivery cost

Delivery costs 6.50 dollars, or nothing for orders over 60 dollars.

## Pickup at the shop

You can pick up an online order at the shop for free. Orders are ready within 1 business day. We keep a pickup order for 14 days.

## Pre-orders

Pre-orders ship on the day the publisher releases the book.
