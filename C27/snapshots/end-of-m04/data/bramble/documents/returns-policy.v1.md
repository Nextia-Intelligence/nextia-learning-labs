# Returning a book

## Which version applies

This policy applies to books bought from Bramble Books online or in our shop in Victoria. The version that applies is the one in force on the date of purchase.

## Return window

You can return a book within 30 days of purchase if it is unread and in the condition we sent it. Bring the receipt or the order number.

## Refunds

We refund the price of the book to the card you paid with. We do not refund the delivery cost unless the book was damaged or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong books

If a book arrives damaged, or we sent the wrong book, write to us within 14 days with a photo. We send a new copy or refund the whole order, as you prefer. You do not need to send the book back.

## Signed copies

Signed copies are final sale. We take them back only if they are damaged or wrong.
