# Refund approval (staff procedure)

## Who approves a refund

Staff can approve a refund of up to 100 dollars. A refund over 100 dollars needs the owner's approval.

## Goodwill credit

You can give a customer a goodwill credit of up to 15 dollars, once per customer per year. Record it with the order number.

## Timing

Send the refund within 3 business days of approval.

## Records

Write every refund in the refunds sheet with the order number (BB- followed by 5 digits), the amount and the reason.
