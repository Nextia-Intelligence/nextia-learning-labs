# Frequently asked questions

## When is the shop open?

The shop is open Tuesday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm, and Sunday, 12 pm to 4 pm. It is closed on Mondays.

## How long does a special order take?

Special orders usually arrive within 5 business days.

## Can I return a book?

Yes, within 30 days if it is unread. See our returns policy for the details.

## Do you have a newsletter?

Yes. Our newsletter comes once a month, with new books and events. You can stop it at any time.

## Can I pick up my online order?

Yes, for free. We send an email when it is ready.
