# Wholesale terms (staff only)

## Our wholesale discount

Our wholesaler gives Bramble Books 40 percent off the list price, and 45 percent on orders of 50 books or more. Do not share these terms with customers.

## Discounts we may give

Book clubs and schools may get 10 percent off. The owner approves larger discounts.

## Returns to the wholesaler

We can return unsold books to the wholesaler within 6 months of delivery, for credit.
