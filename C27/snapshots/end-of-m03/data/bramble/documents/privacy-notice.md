# Privacy notice

## What we keep

We keep your name, email address, delivery address and order history. We keep order records for 6 years, because tax law requires it.

## Card numbers

We never store card numbers. Our payment processor handles every card payment.

## Newsletter

We send the newsletter only if you agree to it. You can stop it with the link in every newsletter.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days, except the order records that we must keep.
