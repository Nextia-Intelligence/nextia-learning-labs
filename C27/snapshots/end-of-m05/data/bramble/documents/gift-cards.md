# Gift cards

## Amounts

Gift cards are sold for any amount from 10 to 200 dollars.

## Where to use them

You can use a gift card online and in the shop, for books, special orders and events.

## How long they last

A gift card is valid for 3 years from the date of purchase.

## Refunds

Gift cards are not refundable, and we do not exchange them for cash. If you lose a gift card, we replace it when you show the receipt.
