# Staff discount (staff only)

## Discount

Staff get 30 percent off books for themselves and their household.

## What is not included

The staff discount does not apply to gift cards, special orders or event tickets.

## Signed copies

Staff may buy a signed copy only after it has been in the shop for 30 days.
