# Special orders and pre-orders

## Special orders

We can order a book that is not in stock if the publisher still prints it. A special order usually arrives in 7 to 10 business days. We send an email when it is ready.

## Deposits

For a special order over 100 dollars, we ask for a deposit of 50 percent. You pay the rest when you collect the book.

## Cancelling a special order

You can cancel a special order on the day you place it, before we order from the publisher. After that, we keep the deposit.

## Out-of-print books

We can search for an out-of-print book through second-hand dealers. The search costs 5 dollars. We tell you the price before you buy.

## Pre-orders

You pay for a pre-order when you place it. You can cancel a pre-order for a full refund until 7 days before the release date. After that, the returns policy applies. To cancel, reply to your order email or ask at the shop.
