# Dataset card: Larkfield final assignment data

Used by: the final assignment of *Forecasting and Time Series: Predict What Comes Next*.

The same synthetic data as [`../../data/`](../../data/dataset.md) (the same history, seed 3535, version 1.1), with two changes:

- a fourth region, **east**, opens on 1 September 2025: it has no rows before that day;
- **40 new products** (P201 to P240) start on 7 July 2025, in every region.

The files and columns are the same. `calendar.csv`, `promotions.csv` and `price_changes.csv` run to 28 June 2026, the end of the quarter that you plan. The sales of that quarter are kept back by the course team, to score your forecasts.
